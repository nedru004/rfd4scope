Trim the target

GPU cost grows with the square of the complex, so cutting a target to the domain you care about is the cheapest speed-up there is. Residue numbering is kept, so your hotspots stay valid.

A binder designed against a trimmed target has never seen the rest of the protein. Check the designs that passed against the full structure for clashes, glycans and membrane orientation before ordering anything.

Review

    
A deeper look at a campaign, to decide which designs to order.

    

      A binder was designed against the construct you gave BindCraft — often a trimmed
      piece of the target — so it has never seen the rest of the protein. Review puts
      every design back on the full-length structure and checks what the construct hid:
      clashes with other subunits or the rest of the chain, contacts running off a cut end,
      a buried epitope, glycans. It groups designs into backbone families, because ten
      sequences on one backbone are one idea and not ten, and shows which binding modes the
      campaign actually found. Without a full-length structure it still does all of that
      except the checks that need one, and says which it left out.
    

    

      Results shows what came out, straight away, even while a campaign is running.
      Review is a build of a minute or two. Everything in it is a prediction.
      A run BindCraft wrote outside this service can be reviewed too: see
      A run from somewhere else, below.
    

    

  


  
  

    
A run from somewhere else

    

      If you ran BindCraft yourself and want only the review, give the run folder’s path on
      this machine. It is linked here where Review can read it — nothing is copied, and
      nothing in your run is written to. Its review is kept with this service’s own.
    

    
    

      If the target structure is not on this machine, or you want another name
      

        

          BindCraft records where the target structure was rather than copying it into the run,
          so a run carried here from another machine names a path that is not here. Give the
          structure and it is kept with this service’s uploads and used for this run.

Full-length structure

      

        The whole protein the construct was cut from. Every design is placed on it, and it is
        what the clash, cut-end, buried-epitope and glycan checks look at. Which one to use is
        not recorded with the designs, so you choose it here.
      


      

        
        
      


      
      


      

        


        

        
        


        
        


        

          Don’t have it here?
          
          
          
            
            
          
          
            
            
          
        

        

          Choose one that holds the whole chain, not another fragment. For an oligomer, one that
          holds the whole assembly is worth having: the other subunits are what the
          other-subunit check looks at, and the chains it found are listed above. An AlphaFold
          model covers the whole chain, but it is a prediction and one conformation, so what
          it finds is advisory; a measured structure is the stronger reference.
        


        

          Advanced

        own review is not in it to begin with. Once it is built, its order list offers to
        bring those over from this browser.

Storage 

    

      What your campaigns and their reviews take on disk, by project. Binderscope reports this and
      archives campaigns; it never deletes results. Anything you decide to remove, you remove
      yourself, from the path shown.
