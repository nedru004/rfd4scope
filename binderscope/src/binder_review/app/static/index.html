Binderscope

Decide which designs to take into the lab.

Binderscope reads your BindCraft runs, groups the designs by backbone family, checks each one on the full-length target and gives you a board to keep, maybe or reject. It runs on this computer only, and it never changes your files.

  1. Choose your runsPoint it at the BindCraft run folders. They stay where they are.
  2. Add the targetThe structure you gave BindCraft and a full-length reference of the target.
  3. Build and reviewThe analysis takes a minute or two, then the review pages open here.
